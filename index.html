How many hours did you sleep last night?
This is easy to lose track of since it's entered the morning after. Skipping it doesn't just leave a gap in your data — poor sleep genuinely blunts what today's protein and fiber can actually do for you: it impairs muscle protein synthesis (so less of today's protein gets used for recovery), disrupts appetite-regulating hormones (making it harder to actually hit your targets today), and is linked to reduced gut microbial diversity over time — the same gut health your fiber intake is meant to support.
Skip for now
QUICK CHECK-IN
How much water have you had so far today?
Even mild under-hydration measurably affects focus, energy, and physical performance — often before you consciously feel thirsty, which is exactly why it's the easiest target to quietly under-track. A couple of taps now keeps today's numbers honest.
